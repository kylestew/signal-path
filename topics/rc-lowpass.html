RC Lowpass Filter

Pass lows, cut highs — the simplest filter in analog electronics

What It Does

Resistor in series, capacitor to ground. At low frequencies the cap is effectively open, so the signal passes through. At high frequencies the cap is a short to ground and the signal gets shunted away.

Why It Matters

  • Smooths noisy signals — strips out high-frequency hash
  • Anti-aliasing before an ADC — prevents foldback artifacts
  • Tone control — roll off treble in audio paths
  • Power supply filtering — clean up ripple on DC rails

The Circuit

Signal passes through the resistor, then the capacitor shunts high frequencies to ground. Output is taken across the capacitor.

Cutoff Frequency

Same formula as AC coupling:

fc = 1 / (2πRC)

Below cutoff, signal passes at full amplitude. Above cutoff, it rolls off at 20dB per decade.

Same Circuit, Different Tap

This is the exact same RC network as AC coupling. The only difference is where you take the output:

  • Across the capacitor → lowpass (this page)
  • Across the resistor → highpass (AC coupling)

Same components, same cutoff frequency, complementary responses.
