RC Lowpass Filter

Keep the slow parts, reduce the fast parts

What It Does

An RC lowpass filter smooths out fast changes. Low frequencies mostly stay in the signal, while high frequencies are reduced.

Why It Matters

  • Reduces high-frequency noise on a signal
  • Softens treble in an audio path
  • Helps clean up ripple or noise on a power rail
  • Can limit bandwidth before an ADC

The Circuit

The signal goes through a resistor, and a capacitor connects the output node to ground. As frequency rises, the capacitor pulls more of the signal away. The output is taken at the junction of the resistor and capacitor.

Cutoff Frequency

The cutoff frequency tells you where the filter starts noticeably reducing the highs:

fc = 1 / (2πRC)

At the cutoff point, the output is about 70.7% of the input. Well below it, the signal is mostly unchanged. Above it, the filter rolls off at 20dB per decade.

Related To AC Coupling

This filter and AC coupling use the same two parts, but they are arranged to do opposite jobs:

  • Lowpass: resistor first, capacitor to ground, output at their junction
  • High-pass / AC coupling: capacitor first, resistor to ground, output at their junction

Same kind of parts, same cutoff formula, opposite result.
